## Prompt

Create an ORIGINAL polished production game sprite sheet for Echoes of Dante, Organic Sci-Fi R3. Transparent background, landscape 1536x1024, EXACTLY THREE columns and TWO rows of equal square cells (512x512). SIX complete images of the SAME quadrupedal Hollow crawler, all facing RIGHT in a steep TOP DOWN orthographic view (75 degrees), with the same scale, identical carapace volume, same lighting, same center and footprint registration in every cell. Each complete creature stays fully inside its cell with large transparent gutters. No text, no grid lines, no scenery, NO shadow baked outside the body. This is a small alien ecological predator, not a boss or humanoid. Reference image 1 is its existing layered mineral-organic shell: preserve layered moss-grey/olive stone-chitin armor, warm bone edges, subtle purple mineral scars, amber/orange living eye/energy cavity near the front facing RIGHT. Upgrade to cohesive hand-painted materiality and volume matching forest reference 2: sunlit warm ivory upper-left edges, cool jade undersides, textured mineral armor with broad readable values and muscular organic joins. Four sturdy articulated crawling legs with blunt hooked toes clearly support the body, powerful forward forelimbs and compact hind limbs, asymmetrical worn shell plates, interesting compact non-spider silhouette. Avoid thin spindly limbs or eight legs, no robots. Creature occupies about 400px wide by 290px high WITH legs within each 512px square cell. Six frames, read left to right: TOP ROW frames 1,2,3 are sequential walking strides, diagonal pair A forward then passing then pair B forward. BOTTOM LEFT frame4 continues walk passing back to frame1; BOTTOM MIDDLE frame5 is attack preparation, all feet planted, head pulled back and front limbs braced outward, amber cavity brighter; BOTTOM RIGHT frame6 is a short forward bite/thrust, forelegs forward, body leaning, orange cavity brightest, NO projectile/explosion. Change limb stance between frames but keep the identical whole shell anatomy, center, size, facing and painterly finish. Complete creature paintings only, do not separate limbs into parts. At gameplay it will display around 100x76 world units. The same distinctive creature in all six cells. True transparent alpha outside each entire creature.

## Resultado e preparação

Saída RGBA 1536×1024 com alpha 0–254. O preview da ferramenta parece ter fundo marrom; o arquivo tem transparência real verificada, sem preencher o retângulo do sprite no jogo. Redução uniforme do atlas inteiro para 768×512 com Pillow/Lanczos, `save(...,quality=94,method=6)`. Células constantes, sem redimensionamento individual pela caixa das patas. A geração tem pequenas variações pictóricas entre poses; não é animação esquelética. Preservar esta fonte ao preparar revisões e não prometer repetição bit a bit da geração.
