# Atlas elementais — origem e prompts

Três atlas originais gerados com a ferramenta interna `image_gen`, com transparência real. Cada atlas contém o projétil à esquerda e a manifestação de chão à direita. Fontes em `source-art/`; nenhuma imagem externa, personagem ou cenário foi reutilizado. Preparação técnica offline com `scripts/prepare-astral-elements.py`.

## fire

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original FIRE ELEMENT VFX ATLAS for Echoes of Dante, a painted organic sci-fi top-down 2D action RPG. Make ONE transparent atlas with exactly TWO separated square cells side by side, no text: LEFT CELL a compact horizontal fireball travelling RIGHT, round brilliant amber hot core, curling orange flame body and a short wispy red-orange tail trailing LEFT, small coherent object silhouette, not just a light blob; RIGHT CELL a localized burning-ground patch seen from a high 3/4 overhead game camera: irregular bed of embers, scattered dark charred cracks, 5–7 organic low tongues of fire rising from the surface, amber centers and dark red outer flames, a grounded low silhouette, not a giant bonfire, flames contained within a footprint. Painting with light/shadow and selective detail, crisp readable at small game scale; world colors #ffbd54 #ff6a4a. Soft translucent flame tips but meaningful painted fire shapes. Each cell centered independently with generous transparent gutters; two equal cells, wide landscape canvas. Real alpha transparent background throughout, no ground rectangle, no sky, no environment, no symbols, no circular magic ring, no characters, no UI, no labels, no logos. These are reusable original game sprites, not a scene, no source from any other game.
```

## earth

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original EARTH ELEMENT VFX ATLAS for Echoes of Dante, painted organic sci-fi top-down 2D action RPG. ONE transparent atlas with exactly TWO separated square cells side by side, no text: LEFT CELL a small rounded irregular mineral stone projectile, chunk of weathered dark slate stone with amber mineral seam, illustrated organic surface, clear volume with light from upper left, no large triangular facets, no fire, little pebble fragments trailing LEFT, travels RIGHT; RIGHT CELL a short heavy irregular rocky outcrop ERUPTING upward from the ground, tilted asymmetrical main rounded stone mass and 2 small associated rounded submasses, broken ground at its base with a few clods and compact dust, subtle amber mineral veins, material and volume like a painted game formation. View high 3/4 overhead suitable for current top-down game. Strong grounded silhouette, rock at the TOP of cell, broad contact base at lower part of cell, not a tower, height roughly same as footprint width. Do NOT make crystal triangles, geometric spikes, giant triangle, polygon pile, a statue, a robot or a magical earth glyph. Each cell independently centered, equal square cells with transparent margins and large gutters in wide landscape canvas. Real alpha transparent background, no floor rectangle, no scene, no text, no figures, no logos or borrowed game art.
```
