A fresh burrow opening made by a very large six-legged digging beast in warm ochre dune sand. Entire hole including rim centered, completely inside image with generous transparent margins, width approx 82% of image, height 46%, centered at image center. Fully transparent background around the hole's feathered irregular sandy perimeter, no opaque square backdrop, no checkerboard painted in, no labels or text. One single hole only, square 1024x1024 canvas. This will be a 240x120 world-unit sprite, so keep a clear broad front lip and coherent surface masses.
