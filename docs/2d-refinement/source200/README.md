# Fontes de produção da revisão 0.1.200

Produzidas com o modo integrado da skill **imagegen**, sem CLI/API ou pacote de terceiros. Referências: pinturas completas originais de Echoes em `public/assets/visual/complete-actors/warrior-*-none-motion-v4.png` e `customization/heads-warrior-female.png`. Nenhuma criatura, arma ou personagem extraído de outra franquia.

Arquivos finais neste diretório: `heads-male.png`, `heads-female.png`, `warrior-side-male.png`, `warrior-side-female.png`, `warrior-action-male.png` e `warrior-action-female.png`. A primeira folha feminina de ação veio com fundo semitransparente; foi corrigida no próprio imagegen antes de importar. A operação de importação preserva alpha, recorta células, remove ilhas desconectadas e registra uniformemente a figura completa. Não fabrica partes ou quadros por deformação.

## Prompts de produção

Especificação comum: **Organic Sci-Fi R3 / identidade preservada**, pintura ARPG isométrica vista de cima, luz superior esquerda, escala estável, anatomia adulta, roupas práticas, figuras completas, PNG transparente, sem texto/bordas/chão/sombras/elementos de franquias. Tamanho final da ferramenta pode diferir do solicitado: o importador lê dimensões reais.

1. **Heads female / heads male — 8 colunas × 2 linhas.** “Create NEW haircuts, not copying the other classes. Each square tile contains one isolated adult head and short neck only, no shoulder/armor/body. Same natural face in all tiles, dark brown natural hair, warm medium skin. Row 1: short rounded textured curly crop, moderate compact curls. Row 2: swept hair with a clearly visible low tied bun behind head. Exact views: front/south, front-right/southeast, right/east, rear-right/northeast, rear/north, rear-left/northwest, left/west, front-left/southwest. Fixed head scale and neutral expression; top skull 20%, neck base 82%. Transparent padding.” Female: natural adult female face. Male: same male identity as original warrior.
2. **Warrior side male / female — 8 colunas × 3 linhas.** “Repair sliding WALK ANIMATION of this exact warrior. Columns 1–4 walk RIGHT/EAST; columns 5–8 walk LEFT/WEST. Four distinct phases: front heel planting/rear toe, passing with rear knee raised/support under pelvis, opposite contact with legs swapped, opposite passing. Actual foot rocker and counter swing, no translated duplicate. One connected full figure per cell, blade held safely downward in continuous grip. Row 1 charcoal shirt/olive cargo trousers/brown boots; row 2 compact light bronze/olive carapace; row 3 reinforced dark steel/olive armor and open-face helmet. Same face, head, body size and ground line. No anatomy assembly.” Female: athletic practical adult woman, short tousled brown hair; male: original young adult warrior.
3. **Warrior action male / female — 8 colunas × 3 linhas.** “Repair attack pose apparent body size. SAME physical height/head/torso/limb sizes as idle reference. Do NOT enlarge torso or shrink head to fit attack. Grounded short attack stance, knees lightly bent, feet shoulder width; compact tech saber in continuous hand grip. No giant squat, wide lunge or rifle pose. Eight directions in the same order as heads; same three outfits as side walk. Every blade fits padded cell. No FX, separate limbs, text, background or shadows.”
4. **Correção alpha feminina (sobre a folha final).** “Remove background completely. Output a TRANSPARENT cutout PNG of the exact 24 female sprites in this atlas. All pale grey/offwhite area MUST have alpha=0, not alpha 160 or any translucent backdrop. Keep foreground sprites opaque and unchanged: all 24 whole women and swords with their original colors/arrangement/scale. Preserve 8columns x3rows. Remove ground shadows too. No tinted white film or checkerboard, every pixel that isn't part of woman or held equipment must be alpha0. This is an extraction operation, no redesign.”

Os prompts acima registram as especificações e o conjunto final usado; os binários finais preservam as fontes visuais. Os quatro estilos anteriores do editor e PixelFX são mantidos com suas origens/créditos anteriores. `scripts/prepare-2d-revision200.py` gera os atlas públicos e `qa/200/import/report.json` registra bytes/hashes reais.
