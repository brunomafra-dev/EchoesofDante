# O Soterrado — arte original

Gerado offline com imagegen, usando o Dune Pouncer do próprio projeto como referência de pintura. Sem assets externos. Fonte com alpha em `source-art/soterrado-source.png`; preparação reproduzível com `python scripts/prepare-soterrado-art.py`.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Original game sprite sheet for Echoes of Dante, Organic Sci-Fi R3. Create ONE coherent original burrowing boss creature called O Soterrado, a living desert apex animal, not a robot, not humanoid, not a worm-only sprite, not a rock with eyes. Anatomy: squat broad six-legged mineral-armored dune creature with a low sand-colored head, two powerful asymmetric shovel forelimbs for burrowing, four weight-bearing jointed rear legs, layered weathered ochre carapace, dark organic tissue at joints, amber sensory pits and small violet mineral seams. Memorable silhouette wider than tall; beast feels massive and grounded. Material: hand-painted stylized game illustration, textured bone/mineral, soft volume, selective detail, warm upper-left lighting. Similar artistic fidelity and painting to the reference sprites, but a genuinely new animal with distinctive digging anatomy. Transparent background, no ground, no labels, no text, no frame lines, no UI. EXACT 4 columns by 2 rows evenly spaced grid, eight cells, one FULL intact creature per cell, same scale, consistent position and same three-quarter overhead view facing RIGHT, no rotations or upside-down figures. Each figure confined completely inside its cell with transparent margins. Cell sequence left-to-right row1: 0 neutral grounded stance, 1 walking step with forelimb forward, 2 alternate walking step other legs forward, 3 attack preparation with shovel forelimbs raised and weight shifted back. Row2: 4 shovel forelimb sweeping forward with body grounded, 5 emerging upwards head and forelimbs raised (full body remains visible), 6 powerful braced phase posture mineral cracks brighter, 7 collapsed defeated pose with limbs resting and head lowered. Do not include particles, dust clouds or copied game assets. Keep silhouette and anatomy identical across poses. Runtime sprite will be around 220 world units across, against a player about 80 high. Reference image serves painting/anatomical family only, not a subject to copy. Output wide sprite sheet 2:1 aspect ratio, genuine transparent alpha.

O spritesheet final tem oito células de 384×384, com linha de apoio comum. Não é skeletal animation; alterna poses, apoio e bob discreto pela distância percorrida. Não gira a criatura para apontar a mira.
