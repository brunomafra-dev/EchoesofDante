Restrained material texture, broad low contrast masses, soft upper-left daylight, ash sediment transitions, ancient dark buried slabs integrated subtly in ground, a few tiny amber mineral seams. Ground is continuous painted terrain, no tiles/repetition/no geometric facets/no photorealism. Lava only at outer top/bottom borders; no molten river across walkable middle. Calm grey ash with amber hints and sparse violet ancient traces, clear gameplay negative space. Entire scene top-down with no sky or horizon, no perspective landscape.
